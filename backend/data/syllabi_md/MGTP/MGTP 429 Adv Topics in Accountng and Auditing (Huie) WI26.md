## **DESCRIPTION** 

Advanced Accounting builds on intermediate accounting concepts and examines complex external ‑ reporting issues that arise in practice. This course will further exam complex accounting topics including revenue recognition, share based compensation,  R&D costs and will also cover equity method accounting, consolidation, segment and interim reporting, foreign ‑ currency translation, and SEC reporting requirements. While this course is oriented toward US GAAP, we will make references to IFRS concepts and highlight key differences. 

The purpose of financial reporting is to provide meaningful information to investors, creditors, managers, and other stakeholders so they can assess a company’s performance, prospects, and risk. Through problem sets and real ‑ company disclosure analysis, students will learn to apply US GAAP and understand how accounting policy choices shape financial reports and the stakeholder assessments. 

This course is generally aligned with the contents of the BAR section of the CPA exam.  However, we will not be covering financial statements of employment benefit plans or government entities. 

## **COURSE OUTCOMES/OBJECTIVES** 

By the end of this course, students will: 

- Become familiar with the standard setting bodies (FASB, IASB, and the SEC) and the process by which standards are set. 

- Able to explain the conceptual framework for complex financial reporting analysis 

- Understand the objectives of financial statements. 

- Have an appreciation of certain key differences between US GAAP and IFRS. 

- Possess an awareness of the lines of service within public accounting firms. 

- Be confident with the contents of the BAR section of the CPA exam. 

- Be able to prepare consolidated financial statements, including noncontrolling interests, and perform intercompany eliminations. 

- Determine consolidation outcomes for Variable Interest Entities (VIEs). 

- Gain a deeper understanding of certain complex accounting issues including revenue recognition, share-based compensation etc. 

- Be knowledgeable of SEC reporting requirements.
