

# **Financial Statement Analysis** 

Winter 2026 PROFESSOR: Gerardo Pérez Cavazos EMAIL: gp@ucsd.edu 

## **DESCRIPTION AND OBJECTIVES** 

Companies communicate with stakeholders through their financial statements. The objective of this course is to teach students how to read and use financial statements to understand firms’ current and past performance, to forecast their future profitability, and to estimate their value. 

To accomplish this objective, the course will provide: 

- Experience and tools for reading and using financial statements to make business and investment decisions; 

- A structured approach to measure intrinsic value using accounting numbers; 

- An appreciation for the value and limitations of financial statement analysis in specific contexts.

Assessing risk from financial statements **Week 5:** Midterm. **Week 6:** Financial reporting risk. **Week 7:** Financial modeling. A financial forecast model **Week 8:** Valuation methods **Week 9:** Valuing a business. Residual income model. **Week 10:** Group project presentations
