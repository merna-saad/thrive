

**MGTP401 – 105326: Professional Seminar: Current Topics Spring 2026, OTRSN 1E106** 

**Instructor: Mark Jewett, CPA, MST Email: m1jewett@ucsd.edu Phone: 925-956-9419** 

This class is intended to give students a window into what it is like as a practical matter to be a tax accounting professional in a multinational corporation. As part of this journey, you will be provided with a high-level explanation of some tax accounting and case law concepts. A few career advice nuggets will be provided along the way as well. This will be accomplished through a series of guest speakers who are senior executives in their companies, as well as studying recent real-life situations affecting the tax accounting profession. You should expect to spend approximately 2-4 hours outside of class time every two weeks for the quarter depending on how much you want to get out of the materials. You will discover when the materials are reread, some of which include documents from real-life legal proceedings, you will learn more and more.
