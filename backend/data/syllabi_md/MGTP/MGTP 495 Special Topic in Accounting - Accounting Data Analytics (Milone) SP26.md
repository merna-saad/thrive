## **Course Description** 

The goal of this course is to provide a basic understanding of recent technologies used in data analytics, machine learning, and more broadly artificial intelligence. The course covers a wide range of statistical and artifical intelligence tools, from regression analysis to machine learning as well as reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on the use of these tools in the accounting profession and on accounting data. 

The techniques and vocabulary developed in this course are useful for: 

-  Understanding high level concepts of machine learning and predictive analytics. 

-  Developing an intuition of how ML can be used in practice and its limitations. 

-  Interacting with data science engineers. 

## **Programming language** 

The course will be taught primarily in the python language, but students are welcome to use their language of choice for the assignments and exams. 

## **Course Objectives** 

At the close of this course, you will be able to: 

-  Run simple regression analysis. 

-  Understand the main concepts of machine learning methods. 

-  Setup and train various machine learning algorithms. 

-  Understand the fundamentals of reinforcement learning. 

-  Understand how neural networks integrate with machine learning and reinforcement learning.

### **1 Attendance** 

**Attendance is required for this course.** Students are expected to be on time and attendance will be recorded at the beginning of each session. Attendance will determine the maximum final letter grade that can be achieved, regardless of the grades received for participation, group case studies, or final case study. The table below explains the correspondance between the number of attended classes and the maximum letter grade. For example, if a student is present for 7 classes, she/he cannot be awarded a letter grade higher than B+ even if she/he receives full grades for participation, group case studies, and the final exam. Letter grades are first awarded based on the grading of participation, assignments, and final exam according to the table above, and will be capped by the student’s attendance. The maximum letter grade of A+ will only be awarded to students that attend at least 8 classes out of 10 (if the grades received for participation, assignments and final exam are high enough). 

|Number of attended classes|Maximum Letter Grade|
|---|---|
|8 or more|A+|
|Between 6 and 8|B+|
|Between 4 and 6|C+|
|Between 2 and 4|D+|
|Less than 2|F|



### **2 Group Assignments** 

Students are required to regularly prepare and submit written assignments (cf outline). The assignments with questions will be posted on Canvas at least a week before its due date. These questions are designed to help students understand the course material. Assignments can be done by groups of maximum 4 students. The assignments should be kept as brief as possible. Assignments should be uploaded to Canvas _before_ the beginning of the class. They will be graded with a numerical grade beween 0 and 100. **Note that any assignment that is not submitted will be counted with the lowest grade of 0.**
