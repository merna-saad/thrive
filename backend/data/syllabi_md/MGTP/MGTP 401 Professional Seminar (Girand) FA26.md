## **DESCRIPTION** 

Less than half of CPA candidates pass the FAR section of the CPA exam.  The goal for this class is to prepare you for the FAR section of the CPA exam.  Candidates for this exam generally spend 100+ hours on the numerous, complex topics.  We will look at a few topics each meeting and do practice questions.  I will share test prep resources and strategies that candidates have used in the past to successfully get through this challenging part of the exam. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course, you will: 

- Know what is on the FAR section of the CPA exam 

- Know how to prepare for the FAR section of the CPA exam 

- Have applied various topics to real world examples.
