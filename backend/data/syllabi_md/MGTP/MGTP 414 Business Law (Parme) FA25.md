## **DESCRIPTION** 

This course is designed to provide you with a general understanding of core business law concepts and provide practical exercises that will help you evaluate how the law influences business decision-making. You will learn the basics of how the law shapes businesses as well as how it can be leveraged in a commercial context.  Specifically, we will spend significant time looking at how the law regulates the behavior of actors within a business, significant issues with respect to contracts, how law can be utilized to create and mitigate business risk, and how legal disputes are resolved.  The subject matter of the class incorporates content tested on the CPA Exam’s “REG” section, but is not comprehensive of tested concepts.  The course is intended to establish facility with core concepts and develop a practical understanding of them as well as the ability to apply these concepts in the real world. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course, you will be able to: 

- Understand sources of law and public institutions that enforce civil law 

- Understand principles of agency and application to businesses 

- Identify and distinguish characteristics of different types of businesses 

- Apply business formation principles to maximize business opportunity 

- Understand how valid contracts are formed and enforced 

- Read and understand basic contract provisions 

- Identify and analyze significant provisions of contracts 

- Apply substantive knowledge of liability and exposure for purposes of risk management 

- Understand the benefits and costs associated with vehicles for resolving business disputes 

- • Apply principles of risk management and dispute resolution 

- Understand different types of intellectual property 

- Understand concepts of credit, secured transactions, and bankruptcy

## **Group Projects** 

There will be two major group projects and one smaller project.  The group exercises will require both collaboration with classmates as well as oral presentation regarding specific aspects of the project.

Students will be evaluated based on my evaluation as to whether you provided a meaningful contribution to the group effort. 

Group Project 1 will require students to collaborate for purposes of creating a business and addressing specific problems and challenges the group’s business will face.  The group will present solutions and a strategy for the business. 

Group Project 2 will require students to apply principles of risk management  and dispute resolution to navigate a unique business dispute.  Each group will be provided specific objectives, develop a strategy for managing risk, and deploy that strategy against another group with conflicting interests. 

A third smaller project on intellectual project can be done individually or as a group.  The strength of your individual contributions to group project work will be considered.  I also reserve the right to consider peer feedback in evaluating your group work.  In other words, your grades for group work depend on both your collective and individual contributions. 

## **Quizzes** 

Quizzes will be administered through Canvas.  There will be five (5) quizzes throughout the course.  Your lowest quiz score will be dropped and only the highest four (4) scores will count toward your final grade in the class. 

You will have until 11:59 p.m. on the deadline to finish each quiz.  The quizzes will be multiple choice.  My goal with the quizzes is to reinforce core concepts.  You may assume that the amount of time spent during lecture on a particular subject matter is indicative of its likelihood to appear on a quiz.  Each student will be allowed to drop his/her/their lowest score.

The final exam will be a combination of multiple choice and essay questions.  I will be testing your ability think critically and apply the material we have reviewed.  You will receive guidance and instructions regarding the format of the exams.
