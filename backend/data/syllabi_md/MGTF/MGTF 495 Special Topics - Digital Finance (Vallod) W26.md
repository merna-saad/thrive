## **DESCRIPTION** 

Blockchain has proven its product-market fit as a foundational technology over the last few years. It will become a ubiquitous technology across consumer and enterprise use-cases within the next few years. The financial sector is the fastest to adopt that revolutionary stack, be it in web3-native or traditional finance businesses. Concurrently, GenAI has taken the consumer space by storm, including for professional use-cases. Yet, the challenges to apply it to financial services at scale remain steep, and expectations remain high. 

The ecosystem needs talent to go invent, build, and commercialize the future of financial services. This class provides a core knowledge and understanding to go do that. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course, you will have acquired a good understanding of core pillars of blockchain and AI in finance, and a critical thinking on use-cases towards separating fads from foundational projects as well as a critical thinking on key considerations for product and GTM. 

These will make you competent to work on digital finance use-cases and opportunities where a solid understanding of blockchain and GenAI will be needed to succeed. Roles you will be able to work in will cover both  business or product capacity, at a financial institution, fintech, or blockchain project.
