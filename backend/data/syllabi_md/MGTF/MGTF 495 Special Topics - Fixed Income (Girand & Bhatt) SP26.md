## DESCRIPTION: 

As a narrative to take us through the world of fixed income we are going to follow the life of an actual loan made to a publicly traded corporation.  This loan originated as an unsecured, investment grade bond. When it started out the yield was low and the prospect for full repayment was excellent. Due to unforeseen circumstances, the bond migrated to junk. Junk bonds are quite different from investment grade bonds – and we will learn how. Conditions for the issuer worsened and the loan transformed again. This time it was exchanged for secured, 2nd lien bonds (also known as an out-of-court restructuring). Eventually, with much kicking and screaming, this loan was transformed by the bankruptcy court into the present form - a diminished package of investment grade bonds, warrants and equity.  The original loan lives on in this new form. 

Following the life, death and resurrection of this actual loan is a novel way to shed light on fixed income categories, capital structure elements and the dark underside of capitalism.
