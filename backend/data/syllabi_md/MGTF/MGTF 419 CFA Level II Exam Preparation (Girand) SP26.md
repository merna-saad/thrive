# **DESCRIPTION** 

This class is for candidates who passed CFA Level 1 exam and are preparing for the Level 2 exam.  The Level 2 exam is widely considered the most difficult of the three CFA exams even though the pass rate is slightly higher than level 1.  The test format moves to a fact pattern format that requires a deeper understanding of the material. 

We will look at each of the 10 major topics areas and explore the level of understanding that is required to pass the 2<sup>nd</sup> level exam.  We will do applied valuation work as tested on the actual CFA exams as well as highlight study habits of successful past candidates. 

# **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this class you will be able to: 

- Prepare effectively for the 2026 Level 2 CFA Exam. 

- • Utilize Bloomberg commands  <EQS> , <CAST>, <DDIS> for real-time context of CFA material
