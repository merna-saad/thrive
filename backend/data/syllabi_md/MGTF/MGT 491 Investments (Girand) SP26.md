## **<u>COURSE DESCRIPTION</u>** 

This course is a survey of the investment landscape. We will analyze the characteristics, returns and risks of a variety of asset classes and the securities within them - common equity, preferred equity, corporate debt, sovereign debt, municipal debt and derivatives. We will employ modern portfolio theory to build a passive, diversified investment portfolio. To augment the textbook, students will make extensive use of an Interactive Brokers (IB) trading account to track self-managed portfolios. We will then abandon portfolio theory with a hands-on dive into security selection. Our end goal will be to develop and analyze investment strategies that maximize returns and minimize risks for investors with medium and long-term investment horizons. We will question market efficieny on a weekly basis and be rewarded for our due diligence.
