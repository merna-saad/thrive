### **<u>DESCRIPTION</u>** 

Estimating the financial value of a project or asset is an essential part of the investment decision process. This course focuses on valuing assets - from an entire firm to a single business project – and understanding the drivers of that value. 

We will cover the main valuation approaches in use today, and provide extensive practice via case studies, as well as discussing the underlying conceptual and practical issues in lectures. The course has the secondary goal of deepening your knowledge of other important areas of corporate finance, such as capital structure, and will equip you with skills and knowledge that every finance practitioner should have. 

**Prerequisites** : Master of Finance Core courses or MGT 408 (Finance Core) 

### **<u>OBJECTIVES</u>** 

By the end of the course students should be able to: 

- Calculate valuation-appropriate cash flows 

- Calculate an appropriate discount rate for the cash flows, considering risk characteristics and firm/project capital structure 

- Understand how growth and terminal value assumptions, and capital structure decisions affect valuation 

- • Choose the appropriate valuation model for each specific firm or project (including the WACC and APV approaches, multiples, and real option valuation) 

- Understand the drivers and the uncertainty of the estimated value 

- Communicate the above clearly and succinctly
