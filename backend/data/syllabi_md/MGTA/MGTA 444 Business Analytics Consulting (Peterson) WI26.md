# DESCRIPTION 

Welcome to our graduate course on Business Analytics Solution Design! In this class, we'll dive into the art and science of creating effective business analytics solutions, recognizing that designing the right solution is both the most critical and the most challenging part of any project. Albert Einstein's famously said, “If I had an hour to solve a problem, I'd spend 55 minutes thinking about the problem and 5 minutes thinking about solutions.” We'll also stress the importance of thoroughly understanding and defining problems before jumping into solutions. You'll learn how to frame problems concisely, work efficiently through the data analysis project flow, and apply proven strategies to avoid common mistakes. Through case studies and hands-on assignments, you'll gain skills needed to craft data-driven solutions that make a difference. 

# OBJECTIVES 

At the end of this course, you will: 

- Strengthen your ability to define and frame business problems accurately before designing solutions.

# ASSIGNMENTS 

Assignments are assigned at the end of each class and due before the next class. 

I – Independent, individual work only. No collaboration or consultation allowed. 

G – Students may work together in groups and turn in one project or assignment for the entire group. 

C – Collaboration with classmates is allowed. However, each student must submit an assignment.
