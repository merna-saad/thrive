### **DESCRIPTION** 

Communication skills in the 21<sup>st</sup> century are of utmost importance. A strong presentation requires skill in sharing your ideas with authenticity, clarity and passion. This course will focus on preparing presentations using clear, effective stories to support your data and to persuade your listeners to action. 

Whether you are presenting to a large crowd or to an individual client, we will focus on communication skills needed to inspire your audience. Using some of the techniques that actors use in training, you will have the opportunity to develop your speaking skills, and to improve your ability to effectively connect with your audience. 

This is a lab class that is highly participatory. Each week you will work on exercises to enhance your performance skills designed to help you give clear and compelling presentations. You will work individually and in groups. Each week you will have readings on relative topics, prepare presentations, provide written feedback to your peers and write self-reflections. You will have a final project presentation to prepare on a subject of your choice, which we will begin to work on class 2. 

### **OBJECTIVES** 

At the successful completion of this course, you will gain: 

- More access to sharing your true authentic self 

- Ease of delivery when giving presentations 

- Increased vocal range and flexibility 

- Organizational skills to share your statistical findings in engaging ways 

- Techniques for compelling storytelling 

- Greater ability to engage, connect and motivate your audience
