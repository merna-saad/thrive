# **Course Description:** 

In today’s lightning-fast global business environment, the ability to communicate strategically— in person and virtually, in a team meeting, boardroom, public forum or a venture capital pitch— is essential for impactful leadership. This highly participatory course equips current and aspiring managers with skills and techniques to deliver strategic communications that inspire trust, build relationships, influence decisions, and drive action. 

Managers are called upon to rally teams, secure resources, pitch ideas, respond to challenges and influence stakeholders. Whether you're addressing a single individual or a large group your ability to present with clarity, confidence, and authenticity can determine success.  The ability to strategically communicate and invite sustained relationships and collaboration through communications are invaluable capabilities of effective managers. 

This course explores not only the external techniques of high-impact presentations—eye contact, vocal projection, gesturing—but also the deeper, transformational skills that distinguish great communicators. You’ll learn to speak from both the mind and heart to directly influence the minds and hearts of others.  You’ll develop confidence in utilizing emotional intelligence and articulating your unique point of view. Innovative techniques drawn from both and theater and leadership training will help you connect with audiences in ways that are strategic, memorable and motivating. 

Through a combination of lecture, demonstration, creative exercises, improvisations, peer feedback, and individual coaching you will develop and refine your communications for a wide range of business contexts, including: 

- Attracting top talent to your teams 

- Gaining stakeholder buy-in for key initiatives 

- Articulating technical content to broad constituencies 

- Motivating teams to embrace new goals or challenges 

- Pitching prospective clients for products or services 

- Securing interest from investors and/or funders 

- Navigating informal conversations or networking events 

Each session will involve development, rehearsal, and performance of key messages using proven templates and coaching techniques. You’ll leave this course more confident, creative, and compelling—ready to lead through standing-ovation level strategic communications. 

# **INSTRUCTIONAL METHODS** 

Each class will include lecture/demonstration and “rehearsal” of key skills and techniques.  The class is highly participatory.  Students will follow script templates and create impactful presentations.  Each class will include developing, rehearsing, and presenting key communications. Instructor and classmates will provide coaching and feedback. 

# **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of Stratetic Communications you will gain: 

- Increased comfort and confidence in multiple platforms for presenting 

- Techniques to draw in, engage, and move audiences to action 

- Confidence being spontaneous and “in the moment” and adjusting to the emotional states and diverse backgrounds of others 

- Experience drawing on authentic interests, passions, and real-life experiences as the basis for strategic communications 

- Practice in key skills including eye contact, gestures, vocal projection, use of imagery 

- Knowledge of templates, techniques and structures for more succinct and articulate presentations 

- Experience in “fierce conversations” 

- Strategies for effective visual materials 

- A working vocabulary to observe and discuss the presentations of others
