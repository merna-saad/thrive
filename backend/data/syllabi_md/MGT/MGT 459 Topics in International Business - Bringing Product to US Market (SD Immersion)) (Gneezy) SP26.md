## **OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVES** 

The Rady School of Management at UC San Diego established a center connecting students and entrepreneurs across borders in education, collaboration, and technology translation into new market opportunities. The U.S.-Israel Center on Innovation and Economic Sustainability (USIC) empowers entrepreneurs in San Diego and Israel to jointly examine and address factors in the success of startup companies, innovation, and sustainability. 

Fostering the collaborative spirit of the USIC, the San Diego Immersion course brings together up to fifteen graduate students from leading Israeli universities and up to fifteen RSOM graduate students to build cross-cultural relationships, meet local entrepreneurs and thought leaders, and apply their skills to a 48-hour business case hackathon, culminating in presentations to an expert panel. 

This class does not offer tangible learning goals such as frameworks, models, etc. Yet, students will have multiple opportunities to learn by doing and observing. As loosely translated from Newton’s third law of motion: you only get out of it what you put into it. 

Students applying to this program must sign a statement acknowledging they have read and understood all requirements, course structure, etc., and commit to participate in _all_ activities.
