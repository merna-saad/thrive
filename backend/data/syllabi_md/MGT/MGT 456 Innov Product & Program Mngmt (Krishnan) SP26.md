## **COURSE OBJECTIVES** 

The Innovative Product and Program Management (iPM) class deals with the knowledge, skills, and competencies needed to become a creative, high-potential Product and Program Manager in the AI age. While we focus on increasingly ubiquitous digitally-enriched products and projects, we also consider management of conventional  products and programs (life sciences and electromechanical offerings) in a digital, high-velocity environment. Key aspects of product strategy,  portfolio and program management, agile product development, and product lifecycle management are discussed, as well as the associated responsibilities of the product manager. This course is intended for students seeking a PM position in either a new or established enterprise and is based on management development programs developed for top companies by the Professor and taught at MIT and UC San Diego. The course augments and enriches content on business strategy, marketing,  entrepreneurship, and operations management- so counts towards Marketing and Supply Chain certificates at UC San Diego’s Rady School.. Class sessions consist of instructor-led discussions, in-class exercises and guest speakers on PM topics. Students work on team-based exercises, and a team-based hands-on PM project in the field with a silicon valley company.

Some criteria used to evaluate class contribution are as follows:

2. Is the participant a good listener? Are the points made relevant to the discussion? Are they linked to the comments of others? Do the comments show evidence of analysis of the case?

## <u>Class Project (with Intermediate Milestones): 30%</u> 

The class project is a key element of this class, in which you will focus as a team on a digital product you like, understand the roadmap and product strategy, identify unmet needs and define a key feature not yet offered in the product, develop requirements, complete a testable prototype, and collect customer feedback. You will learn to use AI-based tooks introduced in the class. At the mid-term milestone, each team will submit their product strategy/roadmap and unmet needs. There will be a project deliverable due the last class session which will count for 20% of the class grade, and will include a prototype tested with user testing resources. As a team, you would present your project test results and lessons learned. There is no final exam in this class.
