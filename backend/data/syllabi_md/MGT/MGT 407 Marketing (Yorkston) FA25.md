## **DESCRIPTION** 

Marketing Management focuses on the strategic and tactical aspects of marketing decision-making in dynamic business environments. Understanding this topic is essential for driving business growth, building strong customer relationships, and staying competitive in a rapidly changing marketplace. 

This is a case-based course. Cases, as microcosms, deliver the power of real-world, applied settings and reveal concepts and frameworks that can inform management thoughts and actions. Our collective experience and expertise outweighs and overshadows the knowledge any one faculty member can impart. Case discussions, and the full participation they require, is an integral component of this course. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of Marketing you will be able to: 

- Analyze market opportunities to identify customer needs and competitive advantages. 

- Develop effective marketing strategies aligned with organizational goals. 

- Apply marketing concepts to real-world business scenarios and decision-making processes. 

- Evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns and suggest improvements. 

- Demonstrate the ability to create integrated marketing plans that deliver customer value.

## **Class Participation (I)** 

Class participation is earned by “attending” class by contributing your thoughts and questions to the various discussions in class.  In practice, great ideas that are not articulated (or are not articulated in a compelling way) are ignored. Thus, participation is important to your continued professional development. Further, much of the value of an MBA comes from the interchange with classmates who come from various industries and strategic contexts. The benefits that each of you receive from this class will be directly enhanced by the mutual sharing of insights.  Thus, throughout the term, I will track the degree to which you contribute to discussion. Please know that quality of contribution (meaningful insights that help us progress in our discussion of the topic) far outweighs quantity. 

## **Weekley Quizzes (G)** 

You will be asked to recognize, apply, and explore the marketing strategies discussed in the readings and the cases. To solidify the knowledge, you will complete group discussion quizzes that will ask you to do deeper analysis or take managerial stances and think deeper about the readings. These are not meant to explicitly test whether you have read that week’s readings, but to answer the questions fully, understanding the weekly readings is necessary. However, when completing these quizzes, you **should not** use your notes and class materials unless specifically noted. You **should not** use the wider internet unless specifically noted. We want you to learn and be able to apply this knowledge; LLMs already know this stuff. Hopefully, these quizzes will keep you “on task” by solidifying the knowledge and keep you learning and engaged throughout the quarter.

## **AI POLICY FOR MARKETING MANAGEMENT COURSE (written with the help of ChatGPT)** 

This policy outlines the appropriate use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the Marketing Management course, ensuring that the technology enhances learning while preserving the integrity of individual thought and effort. The policy applies to all enrolled students in the Executive MBA Marketing Management course. 

## **Permitted Use of AI:** 

- Understanding and Research: Students are permitted to use AI tools to deepen their understanding of course materials and assist with research related to course topics. AI can be utilized to summarize readings, provide explanations of complex concepts, and generate ideas for project brainstorming. 

- Explicitly Allowed Assignments: AI may be used in completing assignments only when explicitly stated in the assignment instructions. These will be designed to specifically leverage AI capabilities in a way that enhances the learning objectives of the task. 

## **Prohibited Use of AI:** 

- General Assignments and Examinations: The use of generative AI is strictly prohibited in the completion of regular assignments and during examinations. Assignments are crafted to assess individual understanding and the ability to apply marketing concepts independently. 



- Discussion Contributions: Participation in course discussions, whether online or in-person, must be free of AI assistance. The value of these discussions lies in the personal insights, original thought, and interaction among peers, which AI usage could undermine. 

## **Rationale:** 

- The educational value of assignments and discussions in this course stems from personal intellectual engagement and the development of individual analytical skills. AI, while beneficial in certain contexts, can shortcut these learning processes and reduce the depth of personal understanding and critical thinking. 

## **Enforcement:** 

- Students are expected to adhere to this policy as part of their commitment to academic integrity. Violations of the AI usage policy will be subject to review under the standard academic misconduct procedures and could result in disciplinary action. 

## **Support and Clarifications:** 

- Students are encouraged to seek clarification from the instructor regarding the use of AI tools in specific instances. The instructor is available to provide guidance on how AI can be appropriately integrated into learning activities without compromising educational outcomes.
