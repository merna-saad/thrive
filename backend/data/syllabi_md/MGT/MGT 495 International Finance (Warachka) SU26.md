## **DESCRIPTION** 

This course is intended to provide students with the necessary foundation to investigate the risks and rewards associated with operating and investing internationally. The course uses a combination of online lectures and in-class case discussions. The lectures develop the theoretical foundations required for case analyses. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

By the end of the course, students are expected to be able to: 

- Utilize capital budgeting techniques in an international setting 

- Describe how exchange rate fluctuations yield transaction, translation, and competitive risks 

- Design and analyze strategies using forward, option, and swap contracts to manage currency risk 

- Design strategies for managing the political risk involved in cross-border transactions 

- Explain the interactions between fiscal policy, monetary policy, trade, and currency fluctuations
