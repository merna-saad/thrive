### **COURSE DESCRIPTION** 

This course provides you with external consulting experience which utilizes and builds upon the knowledge and skills acquired through work experience and in this course. The course enriches the Rady experiential learning component and provides an interdisciplinary approach to project execution and presentation. Projects are sponsored and guided by clients from the business community. 

### **OBJECTIVES** 

- Gain a practical understanding of management consulting via hands-on experience working with a corporate client. 

- Gain an in-depth understanding of advanced management techniques, including project and client management, communication, and team dynamics. 

- Perfect use of business communications skills, oral and written, throughout the quarter, culminating in a final oral presentation and written executive summary. 

- Learn best practices for aligning client expectations with realistic project goals and objectives through effective communication and consensus building. 

- Create and deliver a well thought out and professionally executed final deliverable to the client.

### **PROJECTS** 

A Rady Action Project is sponsored by a company or organization.  A typical project includes the following activities and can encompass a business problem that an MBA can solve. 

- 1) Engaging clients to clarify the problem – interviewing, questioning. 

   - b. Context 

   - c. Background 

   - d. Symptom 

- 2) Structure the problem – fishbone diagram, etc. 

   - a) Determine Cause 

   - b) Revise if needed 

- 3) Analyze research –think of all the possibilities 

   - a) Develop solutions – how does it relate to root cause 

- 4) Select a solution 

   - a) Feasibility 

   - b) Best Fit 

- 5) Ongoing communication with the client throughout, sharing your insights and solutions, and revising as needed 

Teams will consist of 3-6 students who receive project guidance from a project leader provided by the company and mentoring from your faculty.  In addition, teams receive business communications coaching from industry and academic professionals. Students must bring their business skills and professionalism to the team to achieve project objectives. You are representing the Rady brand with our sponsors who are also employers, interested in hiring UCSD students.

### **Written Assignments & Discussions** 

1. **Class Attendance (I):** You will receive 2 pts each week for attendance. 

2. **Class Discussions (G)** : You will also receive 4 pts for participating in each of our in-class discussion and one online discussion (Week 1, 3, 4, 6). 

3. **Team Contract (G):** Outlines the roles and procedures the team will follow throughout the project. 

4. **Project Charter (G)** – Outlines the current client situation (business opportunity or challenge), project goals, scope of work, milestones and associated timelines, deliverables, risk assessment, and the basic terms and conditions under which the work will be performed. 

5. **Work Plan(G)** – The detailed weekly breakdown of the scope of work milestones. WP is for internal use only and **not shared with the client.** You may input your project into ChatGPT and ask it to break down your tasks and milestones for your work plan. Although I will need names and details on the project plan. 

6. **Client Status Reports (G)** – The client status report is due to the client 24 hours in advance.  This document allows the client to stay up to date on the progress of the project.  You will also submit the client status report weekly into canvas. 

7. **Case Assignment (I)-** Review the case _Skills for Tomorrow: A Management Team in Crisis_ and answer the questions; submit two-pages to Canvas. 

8. **Executive Summary (G)** - Approximately 1–2-page summary of project purpose, methodology, analysis, findings, actionable recommendations, and next steps, as applicable. The key is to be concise. 

9. **Works Cited (G)** – Reference listings, providing all sources utilized during the quarter. This document will be graded based on the criteria for proper APA citation. 

### **10. Final Presentations (G & I):** 

Internal Final Presentation to Faculty –Final opportunity to refine presentation before giving final client presentation.  Each team has a scheduled time 

Final Client Presentation – Final presentation to the client. This is the culmination of the project work. Your final internal and client presentation will be graded on your ability to communicate your results clearly and effectively translate them to your audience, your pace and transitions, and the relevance and quality of your analysis and explanation of research, as well as its connection to business goals.

Professionalism is defined as participating actively in every aspect of your project and operating as an effective team member and student. The professionalism grade is drawn from peer and faculty grading.
