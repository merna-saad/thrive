# **<u>Course Description</u>** 

Supply Chains are among the most important and discussed topics at today’s business, board and national policy levels  In our complex and technology-intensive environment, _companies do not compete. Supply chains and industries compete. And countries compete through their supply chains_ . This is particularly evident in today’s environment of tariffs, trade wars and restrictions. Supply Chain Finance and the Financing of operations are fundamental aspects of a company’s successful operations and strategic options. 

The supply chain typically controls most of a company’s costs and cash. Free Cash Flow from operations, Cash-to-Cash Cycles, financial performance, operating margins and supply assurance are major drivers and indicators of shareholder value and sustainable competitive success.  A complex and global supply chain cannot be managed successfully without a firm understanding of this topic. The course takes an End-to-End (E2E) “systems perspective” of today’s global supply chain and will address the critical areas of _operational finance_ (the issues related to the financials and analysis of a company’s operations, cash flow and cost management), and _financing the supply chain & operations_ (including traditional and newer methods, financing estimation, fintech, costs, benefits and risks). Every CEO, COO and CFO must understand this aspect of the global supply chain. **This course is a critical component of a business education and career and is directed at managers and students in all the major business functions** . 

# **<u>Learning Objectives</u>** 

The objective of Supply Chain Finance is to provide insights into 

- the impacts that the supply chain has on the financials of a company. 

- analyzing and optimizing working capital, cash and operational costs, and 

- the traditional and more advanced instruments that are used to help finance the operations of the company. 

while explicitly considering End-to-End supply chain transactions, flow and risk management. The course will provide executives and policy makers, with an integrated business understanding of the operations of a company, the financial levers, the supply chain impacts on the financial statements and shareholder value, and the methods and instruments used in financing the company’s supply chain and operations.

# **Faculty Bio:  Christopher Gopal, PhD** 

Dr. Gopal has over 40 years of experience in global supply chain & operations strategy, execution, and technology in a career that has encompassed industry executive management, consulting and executive education.  Dr. Gopal has held executive positions at several leading companies, including Partner & Director of Global Supply Chain & Operations Services at Ernst & Young Consulting, VP in World-Wide Operations at Dell Computer, as well as executive VP positions at Unisys, Overland Storage and SAIC. His consulting clients have included prominent global and regional companies across a range of industries, and he has also assisted in several start-up companies. 

Dr. Gopal has served as a  Member of the Defense Business Board (DBB) for several years. The DBB provides the Secretary and Deputy Secretary of Defense, as well as other senior leaders, trusted independent and objective advice which reflects an outside private sector perspective on proven and effective best business practices for consideration and potential application to the Department. 

Chris is also a Supply Chain Advisor to the Global Fund based in Geneva, <mark>a public-private organization designed to accelerate the end of AIDS, tuberculosis, malaria and COVID as epidemics. He has also served o</mark> n a White House sub-committee on Manufacturing Technologies 

Chris currently consults with companies, serves as the Strategic Advisor at several small companies, teaches at the University of California San Diego and at the University of Southern California, where he also serves on the Advisory Board of the Global Supply Chain Management Center 

Dr. Gopal has spoken and served as an invited panelist at major industry forums, including the Milken Institute Global Forum, IATA World Conferences, the OMI-OECD Forum, the Harvard Business Review Forum, the USC “Learning from the Pandemic to Improve Healthcare” Symposiums in 2021-22, the Energy Institute, Texas on “Sustainable Supply Chains”, the EU Conference on Monopoly and Security held recently in Brussels, the Panel on _“The Next World System: Can US Trade Policy Make Americans More Secure, Democratic, and Prosperous? ”_ at the National Press Club in Washington,  and recently testified before the Congressional USChina  Committee on Supply Chain security in Washington. 

Dr. Gopal is the co-author of four books on Supply Chain Management. His latest book, “ _Breakthrough Supply Chains: How companies and nations can thrive in an Uncertain World”_ was published June 2023 and has been endorsed by the Council for Supply Chain Management Professionals and been accepted into the Pentagon Library. 

Chris holds a PhD from the University of Southern California, and an MBA from the Cranfield School of Management, UK.
