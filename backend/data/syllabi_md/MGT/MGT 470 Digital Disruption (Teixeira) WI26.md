# **Policy on AI, gen AI and LLMs** 

I will assume all students will have access to and use generative AI and LLMs (chatGPT and its clones chatPDF, LLama, Bard, etc.) as their own personal tutor. Anything incremental is their own work and will contribute to their grades. In practice I intend to use Chat GPT to feed my Harvard cases and ask it for summaries and to answer assignment questions about the case/company. I will tell that to students. And I may start class discussions with whatever the genAI tool spits out. That will be the baseline understanding. Any student that delivers in class or in homework at exactly that level will get a maximum grade of a C- or D. In other words, they just learned basic genAI prompts and to memorize the output. For work handed in, I ask students the courtesy to disclose gen AI as a source like any other source on the internet or otherwise. 

IN SUM, I explicitly allow any use of large language models like ChatGPT or others. Be advised that their accuracy is often poor, so please be careful, as you remain solely responsible for the accuracy, content and conciseness of your submitted answers. I view LLMs as good sources of ideas and pointers, but poor sources of accurate information and VERY poor substitutes for human judgment.
