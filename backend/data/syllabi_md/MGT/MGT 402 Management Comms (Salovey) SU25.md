# **DESCRIPTION** 

In our global business environment of lightning-fast communications, strong presentations skills are an essential capability of management.  As leaders you are called on to rally teams around programs, attract interest and capital in new products, pitch clients for products and services, and attract talented practitioners to your teams.  Especially when so many communications are delivered virtually, there is no faster way of directly influencing large numbers of stakeholders than through effective presentations. 

What are the ingredients of great presentations?  What makes audiences feel engaged, moved, and wanting to take future action?  While much communications training focuses on external techniques such as making strong eye contact, projection of your voice, appropriate gesturing, etc., this class also explores the dramatic distinctions between merely good and truly great presenters.  Good presenters make strong eye contact.  Great presenters make the listener feel honored, engaged, and empowered.  Good presenters are clear and efficient.  Great presenters are passionate and authentic. 

This course defines presentations as: speaking before one or more people with a desire to influence a future outcome.  Obviously in management you are presenting all the time. 

Many of this class’ instructional techniques are drawn from the art of training actors to effectively engage audiences in theatre.  Actors’ time-honed techniques for gaining standing ovations provide a framework for presentations that engage and excite.

Whether you are already a seasoned presenter or one who ranks public speaking as your greatest fear, you will get the opportunity to learn, practice, and present in every class.  Expect to have fun, be creative, take risks, and become much more confident in expressing yourself and your ideas in front of others. 

Each class will include lecture/demonstration of skills and techniques.  The class is highly participatory.  Students will follow script templates and create presentations.  Each class will include developing, rehearsing, and presenting key communications.  Students will receive intensive peer and instructor coaching. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of Management Communications you will gain: 

- Increased comfort and confidence in presenting 

- Knowledge of templates and techniques you can use for more succinct and articulate presentations 

- Practice in key skills including: eye contact, vocal projection, use of images, and appropriate gestures 

- Learning techniques to draw in, engage, and move audiences to action 

- Ability to better structure content, ideas, and images 

- Greater access to and willingness to share authenticity 

- Increased comfort with being spontaneous and “in the moment.”
