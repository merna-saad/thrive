# **DESCRIPTION** 

# **Class Size limited to 35.** 

A defining moment in the history of an organization is how it responds to a highly visible crisis that causes real harm to individuals or communities. In today’s environment of 24/7 communication and global transparency, the need to be prepared and to act quickly and effectively is essential. Organizations must balance their own interests with the public interest. Crises involving Toyota, General Motors, United Airlines, Wells Fargo, the Catholic Church, Michigan State University, and the Boy Scouts of America provide vivid examples of the damage caused by the lack of effective leadership during a crisis.The Covid-19 crisis provides a clear and compelling picture of the damage caused by the lack of transparent leadership and the positive benefits of being honest and open. 

This course will provide you with the opportunity to develop your own crisis management plan as part of a team, to define leadership roles, and to make two videotaped presentations that will be evaluated by your colleagues and me. This course will provide a vibrant, highly interactive learning environment designed to help you experience what it’s like to successfully navigate through a complex and challenging simulated crisis which has the potential to cause considerable loss of public image and trust. Ernest Rady will be a visiting speaker during the third class. 

A collateral benefit will be to identify your most effective communication styles. Students will expand their existing repertoire of communications skills by watching peers demonstrate alternative styles. Student teams will make brief presentations in class in a simulated press conference, assuring that they will receive helpful feedback from classmates and from me on how to improve their content, style, and credibility during a crisis presentation. By the conclusion of this course, you should have a clear idea of your personal strengths and weaknesses and be able to build on your strengths. Students will also see the value of working with a communications coach and in preparing far in advance. This class will provide a safe environment for students who are seeking to improve their public speaking skills in stressful situations, and deepen your confidence to assume leadership roles going forward. 

# **OBJECTIVES** 

In this course, you will: 

Learn how to identify the stakeholders involved in a crisis 

Learn how to balance an organization’s interest with the public interest 



Learn how to provide effective leadership and understand leadership roles in a crisis 

Learn how to develop your own crisis management plan as part of a team 

Learn how to communicate effectively in a simulated press conference 

Learn how to help your organization be better prepared to handle a future crisis 

Develop enhanced confidence to assume leadership roles and seek leadership opportunities.
