## **DESCRIPTION** 

This course explores major corporate reorganizations:  acquisitions, divestitures, and restructurings.  Firms make these strategic decisions in face of major competitive challenges and opportunities to increase shareholder value by exploiting growth and repositioning opportunities.  Studying corporate reorganizations gives powerful insights to how firms adapt to technological change, economic and regulatory shocks, and globalization as well as the roles economic institutions play in promoting competition and mandating disclosures. 

The course provides a framework for analyzing transactions based on in-depth analysis of case studies. 

Prerequisites: Knowledge of the concepts of finance developed in MGT 408, basic familiarity with Microsoft Excel. 

## **OBJECTIVES**

## **ASSIGNMENTS & DELIVERABLES** 

## **Reading Assignments** 

This course is based on in-depth analysis of business case studies.  Students will be expected to read carefully the assigned case study prior to class. 

## **Team Projects** 

Students will form teams of 3 to 5 members to analyze a case study in M&A.  Students can either choose a business study case from a list, or develop a “live” case study by analyzing a recent transaction.  Teams are encouraged to supplement information in the case studies with their own research of the companies and relevant issues.  Teams will prepare a 20-25 minute Powerpoint presentation for the last session.
