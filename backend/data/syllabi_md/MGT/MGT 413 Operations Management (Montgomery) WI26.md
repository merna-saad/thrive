## **DESCRIPTION** 

Businesses of any type or size can benefit from careful analysis of the processes that comprise their operations. This course will focus on how to understand and present a business as a series of processes and how to analyze these processes to improve performance. Students will be introduced to the foundations of Operations Management, with emphasis on identifying how businesses structure operations to align with their strategic goals. Through consideration of a range of businesses, we will identify how each makes decisions to manage operations and gain the mathematical skills needed to analyze and improve these decisions. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course, you will be able to: 

- Identify the tradeoffs companies make between the four product attributes on which firms compete: cost, quality, variety, and response time, for a segment of customers 

- Understand why businesses make certain operational decisions to deliver on the attributes they have chosen to align with their strategic position 

- Create process flow diagrams for company processes in order to conduct analysis 

- Use Little’s Law to relate flow times, throughput, and inventory in a stable process 

- Examine the factors affecting flow time and identify ways to reduce flow time within a process 

- Conduct capacity analysis to identify the bottlenecks and utilization rates of resources 

- Explain and optimize different inventory management strategies 

- Present an in-depth assessment of a single business’s operations that you select for your midterm project

## **Assignments** 

To review the material covered in class and help prepare for the weekly quizzes, there will be weekly homework assignments. You are free to work with classmates on these assignments. The assignments will allow for multiple attempts. These assignments are designed to guide your learning more so than to assess your understanding of the material 

The class also includes a midterm project that will require you to analyze a company of your choosing. The report will be due in Week 6. Late submissions for this assignment will still be graded, but will carry deductions commensurate with the delays in submission. The project will focus on your ability to map a company’s processes for deeper operations analysis. Details for the midterm project will be provided in class and on Canvas. 

As part of homework and class participation assessments, the professor will occasionally ask students to prepare additional analysis of the in-class discussions and games. 

## **Quizzes** 

This class will include weekly quizzes, which will occur at the start of each class to ensure students have reviewed the material prior to class. These quizzes will also allow the professor to understand which topics require additional attention. The two lowest quiz grades in the quarter will be dropped. This will also allow students to miss up to two classes without penalty. The professor will provide an overview before each class addressing which material is fair game for the upcoming quiz.
