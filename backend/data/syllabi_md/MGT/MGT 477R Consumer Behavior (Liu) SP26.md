## **DESCRIPTION** 

Contemporary approaches to business emphasize the importance of adopting a marketing focus (a.k.a. market demand focus) in which customer insight becomes a leading driver of business strategy.  The primary goal of this course is to provide the framework, methodology and knowledge to help you effectively gain customer insight. We train you to think about customer behavior in a disciplined, strategic manner, and connect insight to marketing implementation. In addition, while we will not cover the mathematical mechanics of marketing research, we focus on the high-level understanding of marketing research to help you design and interpret research. You are very likely to be either the designers or clients of marketing research. Being able to ask the right questions and interpret the results in an insightful manner will greatly help the usefulness and impact of marketing research. 

This course will include 3 weeks of in-person sessions, plus online asynchronous materials the rest of the time. Materials in-person and online will include case discussions, lectures, individual and group activities to practice what we have learned. Group projects will require coordination among teammates during the asynchronous weeks.  Every session requires student input, so it is important that you prepare for class and participate in class activities as instructed. 

In-person attendance is required for the in-person sessions. Online attendance is required for the asynchronous material. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course, you will be able to: 

- Become demand-centric and audience-centric in business strategy 

- Develop insight into consumer and client behavior 

- Think like a “scientist of people” when approaching marketing challenges 

- Use the customer insight framework to breakdown levels of insight needed 

- Use behavioral insights to inform marketing strategy and practice 

- Know how to seek, design, conduct and interpret marketing research for insight (for more technical knowledge, refer to courses on marketing research and data analytics)
