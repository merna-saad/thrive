## **DESCRIPTION** 

This course is designed to provide you with an understanding of current issues in business law and provide practical exercises that will help you evaluate how the law influences business decision-making. You will learn the basics of how the law shapes businesses as well as how it can be leveraged in a commercial context.  Specifically, we will spend significant time looking at how the law regulates the behavior of actors within a commercial context, significant issues with respect to contracts, how law can be utilized to create and mitigate business risk, and how legal disputes are resolved.  Students will gain practical understanding of major substantive areas of business law. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course, you will be able to: 

- Understand sources of law and public institutions that enforce civil law 

- Understand principles of agency and application to businesses 

- Identify the characteristics of different types of businesses 

- Apply business formation principles to maximize business opportunity 

- Understand how valid contracts are formed and enforced 

- Read and understand basic contract provisions 

- Identify and analyze significant provisions of contracts 

- Apply substantive knowledge of liability and exposure for purposes of risk management 

- Understand the benefits and costs associated with vehicles for resolving business disputes 

- Apply principles of risk management and dispute resolution 

- Understand different types of intellectual property

## **Group Projects** 

There will be three significant group exercises.  The group exercises will require both collaboration with classmates as well as oral presentation regarding specific aspects of the project.  Students will be evaluated based on my evaluation as to whether you provided a meaningful contribution to the group effort. 

Group Project 1 will require students to collaborate for purposes of creating a business and addressing specific problems and challenges the group’s business will face.  The group will present solutions and a strategy for the business. 

Group Project 2 will require students to apply principles of risk management and dispute resolution to navigate a unique business dispute.  Each group will be provided specific objectives, develop a strategy for managing risk, and deploy that strategy against another group with conflicting interests. 

Group Project 3 will require you to research and apply intellectual property subject matter for purposes of our discussion of intellectual property. 

The strength of your individual contributions to group project work will be considered.  I also reserve the right to consider peer feedback in evaluating your group work.  In other words, your grades for group work depend on both your collective and individual contributions.

## **Midterm** 

There will be midterm exam that you will complete through Canvas.  The midterm will address issues discussed in Sessions 1 and 2.  It will be essays that require you to think analytically and apply the substantive information we have reviewed by writing essays.  You will be given ample time to complete it at home. 

## **Final Exam** 

The final examination will have both multiple choice and an essay component.  It involves at least one case study with several prompts that require you to apply principles you have learned throughout the course.
