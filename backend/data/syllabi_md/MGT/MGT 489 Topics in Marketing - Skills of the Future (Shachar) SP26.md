## **DESCRIPTION** 

Imagine this: you’re about to meet your new colleagues, and someone whispers that every one of them is more intelligent than Albert Einstein. Intimidating, right? 

That’s the reality we’re stepping into. Workplaces will be filled with advanced AIs — brilliant, tireless, and beyond anything we’ve known. Frightening? Maybe. 

But here’s the good news: humans still hold five unique advantages. We can see the bigger picture. We connect through social intelligence and storytelling. We adapt. We make decisions in complex, messy systems. And we still spark ideas no machine can replicate. 

This course is designed not to alarm, but to equip you. You’ll learn to sharpen these skills with practical techniques you can apply immediately. By the end, you won’t just understand these advantages — you’ll embody them. 

So when you walk into that future workplace, you’ll feel ready — not overshadowed, but confident, prepared to thrive alongside AI. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

This course strengthens the human skills that matter most in an AI-driven world. You will learn to: 

- Think more clearly and strategically by seeing the big picture. 

- Manage your career and choices with intention by strengthening your decision-making skills. 

- Navigate change and complexity with confidence by staying adaptive and creative. 

- Build trust and influence by using social intelligence and storytelling. 

- Develop the leadership capacity to integrate thinking, deciding, creating, adapting, and influencing – when problems are ambiguous and stakes are high.

## **CHATGPT AND OTHER AI TOOLS** 

This course encourages thoughtful and transparent use of artificial intelligence tools as part of your learning process. You are invited to treat AI as a **thinking partner** – a resource for exploration, refinement, and feedback – not as a substitute for your own analysis or creativity. 

## **Guidelines for using AI (from the assignment document)** 

**Allowed:** Editing, formatting, summarizing notes, checking grammar, and refining phrasing or structure. 

**Allowed with disclosure:** Using AI to generate ideas, outlines, or phrasing. If you use AI to generate any part of your analysis or phrasing, you must include a short AI Usage Note at the end of your report, as well as briefly state what prompts or tasks you used AI for. Also, explain how you verified, modified, or improved the AI’s output, and provide at least one example of that. 

**Recommended:** Use AI as a **thinking partner** , not a writer – to question assumptions, test reasoning, explore alternatives, or clarify structure. 

## **Purpose of this policy** 

The goal is to help you develop judgment about when and how to use AI responsibly – building your capacity to combine human insight with technological support. This aligns with the course’s broader focus.
