## **DESCRIPTION** 

Operations Management (OM) focuses on the systematic design, execution, and continuous improvement of business processes, projects, and partnerships. In today’s highly competitive landscape, effective operations management is critical for organizations of all types. This course moves beyond the traditional emphasis on cost minimization to explore the fundamental challenges that organizations must address to achieve scalable, sustainable growth and profitability. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the end of this course, you will be able to: 

- Understand operations as a process and how it aligns with strategy. 

- Apply and interpret key operational metrics in real-world contexts. 

- Analyze processes and the relationships among operational metrics. 

- Select appropriate methods for evaluating and improving processes. 

- Understand the role of inventory and its impact on operations. 

- Apply Lean Operations concepts to improve efficiency. 

- Recognize how inventory, capacity, and flexibility help mitigate operational risks.
