## **DESCRIPTION** 

In today’s lightning-fast global business environment, the ability to communicate persuasively and to explain data effectively both in person and virtually is essential. This highly participatory course equips analytical professionals to translate complex technical information and business insights into clear, compelling messages for diverse audiences. 

You will learn techniques for high-impact presentations, including eye contact, vocal presence, effective gestures, and clear, visually engaging slide design. You will also develop the transformational skills that distinguish exceptional communicators: speaking directly from the mind and heart to influence the hearts and minds of others. Through practice, you’ll experience the power of authentic, data-driven storytelling and build confidence in tailoring your message to different audiences. 

Drawing on approaches from communication, theater, and leadership training, the course helps you connect with audiences in ways that are both memorable and motivating. A combination of lecture, demonstration, creative exercises, improvisation, peer feedback, and individual coaching will guide you in refining your data-driven communication for a range of business contexts. 

Each session includes the development, rehearsal, and delivery of key messages using proven templates and coaching methods. You will leave the course more confident, creative, and compelling—ready to lead through communication that earns attention and inspires action.

#### **ASSIGNMENTS & DELIVERABLES** 

**Presentation #1 -** Will be a story that you plan and deliver entirely in class. 

**Presentation #2 -** Starting a key data-driven presentation to create relationship, interest and establish credibility. Due in third session. 

**Presentation #3 -** 4–5-minute-high impact upcoming data-driven presentation including: objective, strong opening, defined relationship, compelling data storytelling, visual imagery and slides. 

All class assignments are C - Collaboration with classmates is allowed. However, each student must present individual work. 

#### **Reading Assignments** 

Are specified in the schedule. 

#### **Team Projects** 

**Team Presentation #1** will be developed entirely in-class during 4<sup>th</sup> session.  Topic chosen by team from 3 options given by professor. 

#### **Quizzes/Exams** 

There are no quizzes or exams, but there are two two-minute presentations, a team presentation, and there will be one 4–5-minute final presentation. 

#### **Class Participation** 

It is essential for each student to use each class as an opportunity to actively develop and rehearse presentations and communications.  Participants will be expected to give coaching and feedback to classmates.  There will be exercises and presentations in every class.
