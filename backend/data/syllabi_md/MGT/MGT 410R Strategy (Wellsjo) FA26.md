## **DESCRIPTION** 

This course focuses on strategic decision making by companies in competitive markets. The approach will combine applied economic theory with empirical examples to develop competitive advantage and game theory frameworks. Classes will consist of lecture and discussion of applications, including case studies. 

## **OBJECTIVES** 

At the close of this course you will be able to: 

- Identify sources of profit within and across industries 

- Understand how to create and sustain a business’ competitive advantage 

- Direct how a company should grow, innovate and respond to changing markets 

- Analyze strategic interactions between competing and complementary companies 

- Shape the nature of industry competition to your advantage 

- Use strategic frameworks to communicate within and across businesses

## **Group Final Strategy Project** 

The main project in the course is an analysis of a company to be completed with your team.  You should think of the final project as a more in-depth and less directed version of the case writeup. The goal of the project is to have you practice the skills necessary to do competitive analysis on your own.  Given that, the structure of the assignment is designed to match the structure of a competitive analysis you will do on the job. 

The first step will be for you as a team to select a company and strategic problem they face. The three components of this project are: 

- (1) a written Five Forces Industry Analysis 

- (2) a written Positioning Analysis 

- (3) Presentation of Problem and Recommendation of a Solution 

The Presentation of Problem and Solution will take place on the last day of class and your team will present a key problem your client is facing and offer a solution to that problem. This presentation should include an executive summary slide and will also include the findings from your analyses where relevant.  Each team member is encouraged to take a role in the final presentation. Additional guidelines, like presentation times, will be announced. 

Part of your participation grade in the course will involve asking questions to the other groups when they present, so please be an active participant.
