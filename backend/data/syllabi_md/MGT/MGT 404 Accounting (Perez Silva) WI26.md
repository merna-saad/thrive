# **Course Description** 

This course focuses on developing participants’ ability to understand accounting information to make business decisions. Specifically, the class provides an introduction to financial statements and the financial reporting process. Participants learn skills necessary to become informed users of financial statements. By the end of the course, students should be (i) comfortable reading and preparing financial statements, and (ii) familiar with common business transactions and how these flow into financial statements. 

Classes consist of lectures along with case discussions intended to illustrate key concepts and provide opportunities to practice recording business transactions and constructing financial statements. Students are expected to complete reading assignments, quizzes, homework assignments, and cases in preparation for each class. This course prepares students for more advanced financial statement analysis courses.
